## Built on the work of others

vpn-forge would not exist without the open-source projects it stands on. Full
licence details are in [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md); the projects most central to it:

- **[Laravel](https://laravel.com)** — the application framework.
- **[Filament](https://filamentphp.com)** — the admin panel toolkit, and its
  community-maintained translations, which localise the framework interface.
- **[WireGuard](https://www.wireguard.com)** and **[OpenVPN](https://openvpn.net)**
  — the two VPN engines the panel drives.
- **[dnsmasq](https://thekelleys.org.uk/dnsmasq/doc.html)** — the per-service
  resolver behind DNS visibility and blocklists.
- **[gopacket](https://github.com/google/gopacket)** — packet capture for the Go
  agent.
- **[GSAP](https://gsap.com)** — the panel's motion layer.
- **[Dompdf](https://github.com/dompdf/dompdf)** — PDF report rendering.

The domain-category and subscription blocklists the panel can consume are
maintained by their own communities — among them
**[StevenBlack/hosts](https://github.com/StevenBlack/hosts)**, **[OISD](https://oisd.nl)**,
**[HaGeZi](https://github.com/hagezi/dns-blocklists)** and **[AdGuard](https://adguard.com)**.
Please support them if you rely on their lists.
